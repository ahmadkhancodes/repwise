# Delete your Repwise account and data

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · Draft for https://repwise.app/delete-account (this is the URL entered as the data deletion link in Google Play and App Store Connect)

You do not need an account to use Repwise. If you never signed in, your data is only on your phone: uninstalling the app removes it, and we hold nothing about you to delete.

If you did create an account for backup, you can delete it and your backed-up data in either of two ways.

## Option 1: in the app (fastest)

1. Open Repwise.
2. Go to **Settings > Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account and backed-up data are removed from our server straight away and you are signed out. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Option 2: by email (without the app)

Email **buffersoltechnologies@gmail.com** from the address linked to your account (or, if you signed in with Google or Apple, tell us which one and the email on that account). Use the subject line "Delete my Repwise account".

We may ask one question to confirm it is you. We will delete your account within **30 days** (usually within a few days) and confirm by email.

## What gets deleted

- Your account (email or Google / Apple id, internal user id) and sign-in tokens
- Backed-up profile, plans, workouts, meals, food logs, body measurements, routines and custom items, and progress photo records
- Anything linked to your account on our database

## What we may keep, and for how long

- **Backups:** deleted copies drop out of our backups within 30 days.
- **Purchase and tax records** (a receipt id, date and amount, held by us and by Apple or Google): kept as long as the law requires, usually 6 to 7 years. They are not used for anything else.
- **Anonymous analytics and crash reports:** not linked to your account, so they cannot be matched to you. They expire within 12 months.

## Your subscription

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings (Settings > your name > Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions), or Settings > Subscription > Manage in the app, before or after you delete.

## Just want a copy first?

Email us and we will send you your backed-up data before you delete.
