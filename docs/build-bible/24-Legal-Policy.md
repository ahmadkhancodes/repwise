| Item | When | What to do |
| Name & trademark check | Before Day 1 ends | Search the App Store and Google Play for 'Repwise', check USPTO (TESS), UKIPO and CIPO (Canada) for conflicts in classes 9/42/44, and buy the domain. If conflict → pick from the backup names: Liftwise, Fitplate, Planrep. |
| Privacy policy | Before closed test | Plain-English policy: what is stored on device, what is backed up (only if signed in), processors (Cloudflare, MongoDB Atlas, Apple, Google, AI provider, Cloudinary, PostHog, Sentry, Google), no selling of data, no ads, retention, deletion, contact. Covers UK GDPR, PIPEDA (Canada) and US state privacy laws (e.g. CCPA). |
| Terms of use | Before closed test | Subscription terms, auto-renewal, cancellation via App Store / Google Play, acceptable use, medical disclaimer, limitation of liability. |
| Account deletion (Apple 5.1.1(v) + Play requirement) | Before first submission | In-app path (Settings → Account → Delete) AND a web page where users can request deletion without the app. Enter the web URL in Play Console → Data deletion. |
| Data safety form | Before closed test | Data collected: health & fitness (workouts, body weight, measurements), nutrition logs, diet preferences/allergies, progress photos (private, optional), account email. Processed by our servers and our AI provider (no training on user data — enterprise/API terms). Not sold; no ads; no tracking (no ATT). Deletion in-app + web. |
| Content rating | Before closed test | Complete IARC questionnaire honestly. No user-generated public content, no gambling. Expect 'Everyone' or 'Teen'; set target audience to 18+ in Play Console; Apple age rating 12+ (health/fitness info, no mature content). |
| Subscriptions policy | Always | Paywall disclosures listed in sheet 16. No misleading free-trial claims. Cancellation explained. |
| Health & nutrition disclaimer | Before submission | In onboarding + settings: general guidance, not medical advice; consult a doctor if pregnant, diabetic, injured or with eating-disorder history. Screen out unsafe goals (very low calories, BMI < 18.5 weight-loss goal) and minors (<16). |
| AI disclosure | Before submission | Tell users plans are AI-assisted; privacy policy names the AI provider; no sensitive data in prompts beyond what's needed (no names/emails). |
| Content licences | Before import | Every exercise/food/recipe/photo source has a recorded licence (sheet 29). |
| Disclaimer text (onboarding + Settings → About + store listing) |  |  |
| Repwise provides general fitness and nutrition guidance for healthy adults. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a medical condition. |  |  |
