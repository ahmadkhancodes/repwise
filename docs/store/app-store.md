## Description

```
Repwise plans your training and your food every day, in one app.

Answer a short quiz about your goal, equipment, schedule and food preferences and Repwise builds your AI-personalized gym plan and your daily meal plan. Every week it adapts to what you actually did, so you always know what to lift and what to eat today.

AI-PERSONALIZED GYM PLANS
• A weekly plan built around your goal, equipment and available days
• Fresh sessions each week with smart progression, so you are never stuck repeating the same workout
• Fast gym log: log a set in one tap, with your previous weights pre-filled
• Rest timer with a notification, plate calculator and 1RM calculator
• Exercise library with search, filters, muscle info and looping demos
• Build your own routines and custom exercises

AI-PERSONALIZED MEAL PLANS
• Daily meals in the cuisines you like, matched to your calorie and protein targets
• Respects your diet type, allergies and foods you dislike
• Swap any meal in one tap
• Ate out? Log it and Repwise rebalances the rest of your day
• Step-by-step recipes with cook mode
• Weekly grocery list grouped by aisle, tick items off or share it as text
• Water tracking and quick meal logging

REMINDERS
• Morning brief with today's workout and meals
• Reminders for workouts and meals, on the times you choose, with quiet hours
• Weekly check-in that adjusts your targets and plan

PROGRESS
• Personal records, estimated 1RM charts and workout history
• Muscle heatmap that shows what you trained recently
• Weight trend and body measurements
• Private progress photos with a side-by-side compare slider, kept on your device
• Monthly Wrapped recap of your training

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO LIFT
• Works offline: your plan, your log and your workout player do not need a connection
• No ads, ever
• Account is optional: use Repwise without signing in, and add an account later only if you want backup and restore across devices
• Light and dark themes
• Large touch targets and support for larger text sizes

HOW AI IS USED
Your plan is personalized with AI through our own server. Your targets and the math behind sets, reps, calories and macros come from tested rules, and the AI helps choose and order sessions and meals from a vetted library. We do not send your name or email to the AI. If the AI is unavailable, Repwise falls back to a rules-based plan.

REPWISE PRO
Free: the quiz, your first personal plan, workout logging, the exercise library, 3 routines and basic progress.
Pro: AI plans that adapt every week, a daily meal plan with swaps, grocery list, the full reminder schedule, full progress history and Wrapped.
Try Pro free for 7 days on the yearly plan. Yearly, 3-month, monthly and weekly plans are available. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled and you can cancel any time in your Apple ID subscription settings. The paywall always has a visible close button, and the core logging features stay free.

PRIVACY
Your data lives on your device first. If you create an account, your plans and logs are backed up over an encrypted connection and you can delete your account and data any time from Settings, or on our website. We do not sell your data and there are no ads.

Repwise provides general fitness and nutrition guidance for healthy adults. It is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a medical condition.

Questions or ideas? Email buffersoltechnologies@gmail.com. We read everything.
```

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes")

```

How to test without an account:
1. Launch the app and tap "Get started" on the welcome screen. (Do not tap "I already use Repwise".)
2. Answer the 15 short quiz questions (any answers work). On the notification screen you may tap "Not now".
3. The app builds a personal plan on the device and opens the Today screen. Everything works offline.
4. Try: Today > tick a meal; Train tab > "Start workout" > log a set with one tap; Eat tab > swap a meal and open the grocery list; Progress tab > heatmap and charts.

Where the paywall is:
- Right after the plan preview at the end of onboarding (the plan screen shows the paywall).
- Later: Settings > Subscription, or tap any Pro-labelled feature.
- The paywall has a visible close (X) button at the top, shows price, billing period and the 7-day free trial on the yearly plan (with the price after the trial), Restore Purchases, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links. Closing it continues in Free mode.

Account deletion: Settings > Account > Delete account (also available on the web at the URL in the Privacy Policy). Sign in with Apple is offered next to Google and email sign-in.

AI: plan personalization runs on our server (it calls an AI provider). No name or email is sent. If AI is unavailable the app falls back to a rules-based plan, so review is not affected.

Permissions: notifications (reminders, asked after onboarding), camera and photo library (optional, progress photos only; photos stay on the device). No location, no contacts, no tracking.

Contact: buffersoltechnologies@gmail.com
```
