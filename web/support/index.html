Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Repwise without signing in, and your plans, workouts, meals and progress stay on your device. An account is optional and only for backup, restore and sync across your devices, and for keeping Pro across devices.

How do I change my goal, equipment or diet?

Open Settings and update your profile. Your next plan is built around the new answers. Your logged workouts and meals are kept.

Does Repwise work offline?

Yes. Your plan, your log and the workout player do not need a connection. You need one for things like building a new plan or backing up.

I got a new phone. How do I move my data?

Sign in on your old phone first so your data is backed up, then sign in with the same account on the new phone to restore it. Without an account, data stays only on the old phone. Progress photo files are not uploaded in this version, so export any photos you want to keep.

Plans and AI

How is AI used?

Your plan is personalised with AI through our own server. Your targets and the maths behind sets, reps, calories and macros come from tested rules, and the AI helps choose and order sessions and meals from a vetted library. We do not send your name or email to the AI. If the AI is unavailable, Repwise falls back to a rules-based plan.

A workout or meal does not suit me. What can I do?

Swap any meal in one tap, and change or skip any exercise. You decide what to do with a plan. If you have a medical condition, an injury or an allergy, check with a qualified professional and always check ingredients yourself.

Why did my plan change this week?

The weekly check-in looks at what you actually did and refreshes your sessions, targets and menus for the week ahead.

Subscription and billing

What does Repwise Pro include?

AI plans that adapt every week, a daily meal plan with swaps, the grocery list, the full reminder schedule, full progress history and Wrapped. The quiz, your first personal plan, workout logging, the exercise library, 3 routines and basic progress stay free.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. After the trial it renews at the yearly price shown unless you cancel before it ends. We send a reminder two days before the trial ends.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple or Google. On iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store > profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. In the app, Settings > Subscription > Manage opens the same place. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. Request one from the store you bought from.

I paid but Pro is not unlocked.

Tap Restore purchases on the paywall or in Settings, and make sure you are signed in to the same store account you bought with. If it still does not unlock, email us with the store you used and the purchase date.

Privacy and data

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Or email us from the address linked to your account. Full steps are on the delete account page.

Where are my progress photos stored?

In the app's private storage on your device. They are never saved to your gallery and are only shared if you tap Share.

Can I turn off analytics?

Yes, in Settings. Analytics are anonymous and tied to a random install id, not to your name or email. See the Privacy Policy.
