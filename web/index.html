How AI is used

AI picks. Tested rules do the maths.

  1. You answer a short quiz

    Goal, equipment, schedule, diet type, allergies and the foods you like.

  2. Rules set your targets

    Sets, reps, calories and macros come from our own tested rules, not from the AI.

  3. AI chooses and orders

    Through our own server, AI helps pick sessions and meals from a vetted library. We do not send it your name or email.

  4. Always a fallback

    If the AI is unavailable, Repwise uses a rules-based plan, so you always have a plan for today.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? Visit support or email us. We read everything.

Is Repwise free?

Yes. The quiz, your first personal plan, workout logging, the exercise library, 3 routines and basic progress are free, with no ads. Repwise Pro adds weekly adapting plans, the daily meal plan with swaps, the grocery list, the full reminder schedule, full progress history and Wrapped.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. After the trial it renews at $59.99 a year unless you cancel before it ends, and we send a reminder 2 days before. Cancel any time in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Repwise without signing in, and your plans, workouts, meals and progress stay on your device. An account is optional and only for backup, restore and sync across your devices, and for keeping Pro across devices.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes. Your plan, your log and the workout player work offline. You only need a connection for things like building a new plan or backing up.

What equipment do I need?

Whatever you have. The quiz asks about your equipment and the days you can train, and your plan is built around them. You can also build your own routines and custom exercises.

Can it handle my diet and allergies?

Your meal plan respects the diet type, allergies and disliked foods you enter, and you can swap any meal in one tap. Always check ingredients yourself if you have an allergy.

Will there ever be ads?

No. There are no ads in Repwise, in the free tier or in Pro, and we do not sell your data.

How do I cancel or delete my account?

Cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. To delete your account and backed-up data, go to Settings > Account > Delete account in the app, or follow the steps on our delete account page.

Is Repwise medical advice?

No. Repwise provides general fitness and nutrition guidance for healthy adults aged 18 and over. Consult a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or diet program, especially if you have a medical condition.
